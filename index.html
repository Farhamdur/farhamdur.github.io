Trustworthy and robust AI

Md Farhamdur Reza

Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical & Computer Engineering at NC State University

I develop methods to evaluate and improve the robustness, safety, and controllability of vision, multimodal, and distributed AI systems. My work spans geometric black-box attacks, transferable adversarial examples, multimodal jailbreak analysis, model alignment, and decentralized federated learning.

Research

Evaluating and improving trustworthy AI

My research develops methods for understanding vulnerabilities and improving robustness, safety, and learning under real-world constraints.

Research themes

Five connected areas

Adversarial robustness

Query-efficient geometric black-box attacks for evaluating image classifiers through local decision-boundary structure.

Generative transferability

Generative targeted attacks designed to transfer across unseen models using ensemble and target-sample structure.

Multimodal AI safety

Jailbreak evaluation for large multimodal models, with emphasis on concealment, reconstruction, and safety failure modes.

Alignment and post-training

Parameter-efficient post-training pipelines for studying model controllability, refusal behavior, and alignment drift.

Decentralized federated learning

Learning under mobility, distribution shift, network constraints, and catastrophic forgetting.
